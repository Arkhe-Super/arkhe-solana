# C2PA as ISO 22144

Content Credentials (C2PA) provide tamper-evident metadata detailing content provenance. Arkhe's bridge verifies the trust assertions of C2PA manifests and anchors the hash (RecordHash) as an immutable record on Solana (via WormGraph) or Ethereum (via EAS) to provide a legal foundation under international standards. This allows for verifiable and machine-readable origin tracking.
